# Privacy Policy - AeroRotor Glass Pulse

**Effective Date:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile application **AeroRotor: Glass Pulse** ("the Application"). The Application is designed as an offline precision arcade game.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
AeroRotor: Glass Pulse does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) from its users. All gameplay data, high scores, preferences, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Data Storage & Permissions
The Application operates completely offline and does not require internet connectivity, account registration, or access to sensitive device permissions (such as camera, contacts, microphone, or precise location services).

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Application does not integrate third-party analytics trackers or data collection SDKs that profile users.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our Application complies with global privacy protection standards, including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR. Since we do not collect any personal data, the Application is completely safe for users of all age groups, including children under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
